# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a place where a first-year student in Lagos, a
postdoc in Heidelberg and a lecturer in Manila can all ask a "basic" question
without being made to feel stupid. Computational life sciences has a steep
enough learning curve without adding contempt.

## Expected behaviour

- Assume the question is honest and the asker is capable.
- Critique the code, the data, the citation — never the person.
- State uncertainty explicitly ("I think", "I have not verified") rather than
  sounding confident.
- Respect that contributors may have limited bandwidth, unreliable electricity
  or expensive data. Do not shame someone for a slow response.
- When you are wrong, say so in the thread. It is the most useful thing a
  senior person can model.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or sustained disruption.
- Discriminatory comments about gender, gender identity, sexuality, disability,
  neurotype, appearance, race, ethnicity, nationality, or religion.
- Sexual attention or imagery; deliberate intimidation; doxxing.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- **Scientific misconduct**: presenting fabricated data, results or citations;
  manipulating an assessment answer key; misrepresenting authorship.
- Taking credit for another contributor's work.

## Scope

Applies in all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions, review
comments, and any private communication that arises from project work.

## Reporting

Report to the project contact address published in the repository's
`SECURITY.md` (the maintainer will replace this placeholder before public
launch — **no contact address is invented here**). Reports are handled
confidentially; the reporter will be told what action was taken.

## Enforcement

| Severity | Response |
|---|---|
| Minor (tone, interruption) | Private note, no record kept |
| Repeated minor | Warning; temporary restriction from the repository |
| Serious (harassment, misconduct) | Removal from project spaces; contribution privileges revoked; named in the maintainer log (privately) |
